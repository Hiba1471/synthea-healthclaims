The dearest hospitals charge ordinary prices — their patients come back fifty times

And the ones that really do charge more are mostly hospices, where a whole stay is billed as one visit. Two different problems, one big number.

The hospices are not overcharging. They sit far right because a whole multi-week stay is billed as a single encounter, so their “price per visit” is really a price per stay — the same effect recorded in delayed_claims_tail_2020_2024.csv. Read that cluster as a measurement artefact, not a negotiating target.

Both highlighted groups are identified by their names, because nothing in the data marks a facility as a VA site or a hospice. Six of the twelve say Vet Center or VA Medical Center outright; three others are VA community clinics recognisable only from the naming convention. All twelve sit above 24 visits per patient, which supports the grouping without proving it.
